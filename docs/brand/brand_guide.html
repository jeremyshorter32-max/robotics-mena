Machines in the field.
Evidence on the record.

A bilingual, data-led identity for an independent regional record of robotics, physical AI and automation. Rooted in the selected Articulated Arm + Frame direction.

01 / CORE SIGNATURE

One emblem. Two languages.

PRIMARY / LIGHT
PRIMARY / DARK
ENGLISH / LTR
ARABIC / RTL

The Latin and Arabic wordmarks are equal-status signatures. In English contexts the emblem leads on the left; in Arabic contexts it anchors the right. Do not mirror the robotic arm itself—only the placement of the symbol within the RTL composition.

02 / SYMBOL CONSTRUCTION

The articulated regional marker

Frame = the defined MENA coverage and consistent editorial system. Arm = recognisable real-world industrial robotics, not a generic technology node. Point = verified deployments, tied to a date and primary source. The open frame suggests an expanding record rather than a closed badge of authority.

07 / EDITORIAL SYSTEM

02 / DEPLOYMENTS

03 / POLICY & DATA

Progress, in context.

Use structured comparison tables, footnoted numbers, country codes, and restrained data teal.

Use thin rules, near-square corners, 12-column responsive grids and documentary robotics photography. Replace the earlier decorative khatam footer graphic with subtle crops of the new engineered frame. Do not use mascot robots, generic circuits, stock CGI, country flags, or neon glows.

08 / GOVERNANCE

Usage and handoff

Minimums: icon ≥16px with micro variant; symbol + wordmark ≥140px wide in standard digital interfaces; free space around the logo at least one-third of symbol height. Do not stretch, rotate, reflect, outline, shade, or recolour the mark arbitrarily.

The provided SVG wordmarks retain editable text with local font fallbacks; use the designated Readex Pro font for production and convert final approved wordmarks to outlines before print or external distribution. This system is a working original artwork and has not undergone trademark clearance. Arabic kerning and final optical balance should receive native-speaker typesetting review before formal launch.

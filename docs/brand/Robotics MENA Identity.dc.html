ROBOTICS MENA · ROUND 2 · PHASE 2: STRATEGIC FOUNDATION
Brand truth: the only organisation that sees the whole MENA robotics ecosystem in one place, in both of the region's languages. That means every company, deployment, policy and event.
CATEGORY LANDSCAPE
Robotics media and manufacturers: blue gradients, circuit nodes, robot heads, gears. Red, orange and yellow already belong to ABB, KUKA and Fanuc. Certification bodies (TÜV, UL, DNV, BSI): blue or red seals around initials. Regional tech brands: Latin first, Arabic added as a translation, plus map outlines, falcons and domes.
WHITE SPACE
1. True parity between the two scripts: nobody in robotics designs Arabic and Latin as equals. 2. A symbol with no robot imagery that can be stamped onto hardware. 3. Deep, warm colours outside the manufacturers' red, orange and yellow and the industry's default blue.
THE SINGLE IDEA
The reference point.
Today it is where the ecosystem gathers. In 30 years it is the standard the industry is measured against. The mark has to work as a meeting place now and as a seal later.
DESIGN PRINCIPLES · EVERY TERRITORY IS JUDGED AGAINST THESE
01 Seal-ready
Must still work when stamped, engraved, or laser-etched 8 mm wide onto a robot's casing.
02 Two scripts, one mark
Arabic and Latin are designed as equals. Neither is a translation of the other.
03 No robots
No faces, arms, gears, circuits or connection dots.
04 No geography
No maps, flags, domes or falcons. A global standard has to outgrow the word "MENA".
05 Constructible
Drawn from circles and squares on a 64-unit grid, so anyone with a compass can reproduce it.
06 Silhouette first
Recognisable as a solid black shape at 16 px.
PUSHBACK · ARABIC NAME
The full translation, روبوتات الشرق الأوسط وشمال أفريقيا, runs to five words against two in English. Don't force it onto one line at equal size. Set it the way the English is set: روبوتات large with the region underneath, mirroring "Robotics" over "MENA".
PUSHBACK · "MENA" IN A GLOBAL STANDARD
TÜV, Lloyd's and DNV kept local names and still became global authorities, but their symbols were never local. So the symbol has to carry the global ambition, and that is why principle 04 is non-negotiable.

RATIONALE
The Economist's red and National Geographic's yellow frame show that a field of colour can be the whole brand. Your main surfaces are the brief, social posts and the stage. A green-teal plate carrying both names is recognised before anyone reads it.
FORM
A 2:1 rectangle. Latin sits top-left and Arabic top-right, each set from its own reading edge, with a hairline between them. There is no symbol: the plate is the mark.
TYPOGRAPHY
Alexandria SemiBold and Bold: geometric, with Arabic and Latin drawn by the same designer.
COLOUR
This green-teal sits between the manufacturers' yellow and orange and the corporate blues. It reads as Gulf water rather than a national flag's green.
SCALE
Without a symbol, the favicon fails. The fallback is a green-teal square with a hairline, and it is only recognisable after years of exposure to the plate.
2075
It survives through consistent use, not through its form.
RISKS
It can't be meaningfully engraved, and it fails the silhouette and one-colour tests. It is the right answer for a pure media brand and the wrong one for a future certification body.
PHASE 4: RECOMMENDATION
Build 2a Origin. It is the only territory that passes all nine tests and serves both futures you described.
Why it wins. Today the origin is the zero point, where the whole ecosystem meets. In 30 years it is the reference every robot is measured against. The meaning grows into the ambition instead of having to be replaced. It carries no robot imagery and no geography, so it can outgrow "MENA" the way TÜV outgrew Rheinland.
What to take from the others. From 2d, the struck punch shape becomes the container for the "Robotics MENA Certified" mark, with Origin inside it. From all of them, one rule: both scripts start at the symbol and read outward. Origin is the signature, and Hallmark is how it signs things.
Kill. 2e Plate fails four tests and has no future as a seal. 2c Ra only goes forward with a calligrapher, because the idea is right and the drawing is everything. 2b Majlis is the honest alternative if you decide hospitality matters more than authority.

05 · RULES
Clear space. Leave one shoulder-joint diameter on every side.
Minimum size. 16 px for the symbol, 24 px height for a lockup.
One colour. Indigo on paper, paper on indigo, or black. No gradients, outlines or shadows.
Never redraw. The arm's pose and the outlined wordmark are fixed. Always use the files.
Bilingual. Arabic sits left of the symbol and English right, both reading outward from the arm.

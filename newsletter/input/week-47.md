## Spotlight
title: ADNOC's real AI story at ADIPEC wasn't the OpenAI deal. It was the robots.
body: The ADIPEC headlines went to ADNOC's OpenAI agreement. The bigger shift was on steel. After strikes on Shah, Habshan and Ruwais, every damaged metre of pipe and vessel has to be checked before it carries pressure again, and ADNOC is buying robots to do it: Gecko's wall-climbing crawlers under a contract worth up to AED 110 million, an explosion-proof inspector at the Taweelah gas compression plant, two 60-metre remotely operated vessels steered from Mussafah, and a trial of Unitree robots in onshore fields. Most of the figures attached are company claims, projections or contract ceilings. Gecko says its pilot raised asset coverage by 99.6%, and ADNOC Gas projects more than $300 million in savings over five years. Neither has been independently tested.

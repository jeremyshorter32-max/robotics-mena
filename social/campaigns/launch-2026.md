Content Library page, with copy buttons and downloads.

## Why the first two weeks matter
New accounts with no posts and no followers convert badly: people look, see an empty profile and leave. So the order is:
1. **Fill the profiles before anyone arrives** (Sun 15 Nov): 3 Instagram posts so the grid isn't empty, an X hello post, a LinkedIn cross-post.
2. **Bring the first few hundred people ourselves** (Mon 16 to Fri 20 Nov): your personal post, page invites, the waitlist email, and one
   message to each company we feature. These people follow because they know us or we featured them, and their reshares bring the next wave.
3. **Earn the rest with consistency** (from week 48): the weekly rhythm, daily replies, and GITEX.

## GITEX plan
- **Before:** preview on LinkedIn and X (3 Dec), the UAE-in-numbers carousel (5 Dec; counts are refreshed when it's copied into week 49),
  the eve post and the Reddit megathread (6 Dec). Exhibitors who reply with their stand go on your route.
- **During:** one X live thread a day (the highest-growth format at a live event), Instagram Stories through the day, and one Reel a day
  if the footage allows. The Community agent's sheet that week lists official GITEX and exhibitor posts to reply to.
- **After:** the LinkedIn recap carousel (14 Dec), a "GITEX in 30 seconds" Reel, and every verifiable deployment added to the database through
  portal lead requests, each with its source.
- **Verification on the floor:** a demo is not a deployment. Only post "deployed at X" when the exhibitor says it's live and public (and say "according to
  <company>"). Everything else is "on show", not "deployed".

## Shot list for GITEX and Big 5 (phone, 4K, vertical first)
1. **The robot doing its job,** 5 to 10 seconds, steady, no talking. The most useful shot by far.
2. **The same robot horizontally,** 5 seconds, for X and LinkedIn.
3. **A close-up of the mechanism** (gripper, sensor, wheels), 3 to 5 seconds.
4. **The stand sign or screen** with the company name, for captions and credit.
5. **A 15-second answer from the exhibitor** to "Where in the region is this working today?" Ask before filming and get their name and role.
6. **A wide shot of the crowd at the stand,** with no close faces.

Rules: ask before filming people, never film children, and follow the event's media rules. If an exhibitor asks you not to post, don't.
Send the clips with a note saying which company and stand each one is.

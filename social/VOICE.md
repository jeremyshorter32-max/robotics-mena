# Voice and style for social posts

We sound like a sharp trade-desk editor: precise, calm, specific and a little dry. Never like a hype account.

## Do
- **Lead with the fact or the number.** "Uber expects ~200 driverless taxis on UAE roads by year-end." not "Big news for autonomy! 🚀"
- **Say why it matters in one line.** Every signal post answers "so what?" for an operator, buyer or investor.
- **Name names.** The company, operator, site, city and date. Tag the organisation's handle where it has one.
- **Cite.** Put "Source: <outlet or company>" on every factual post, and the link where the platform allows it (on LinkedIn, in the first comment).
- **Use plain words.** "Robot that climbs pipes to measure wall thickness" before "ultrasonic crawler NDT".
- **Compare across the region.** "Saudi Arabia: 17 deployments in our database. UAE: 26. Egypt: 6."
- **English only for now.** Posts, captions, cards and replies are in English (owner's decision, 14 Nov 2026). Where a company has an
  Arabic name, use its English name as the site does. Arabic may be added later (`social/cadence.json` languages).

## Don't
- Don't hype: no "revolutionary", "game-changer", "the future is here", 🚀🔥🤯, or "you won't believe".
- Don't post engagement bait: no "comment YES", "tag 3 friends" or "follow for part 2".
- Don't present guesses as facts. If a figure is company-reported, say "company says". If a date is a target, say "target".
- Don't post politics or take sides on conflicts. Cover what robots do, not who should win.
- Don't mention or show Israeli companies, the Israeli flag or Israeli sources. Don't cover defence or weapons makers (pending the owner's decision).
- Don't sound like an AI: no "In today's rapidly evolving landscape", "delve", "it's worth noting", "Let's dive in".

## Platform style

| | Length | Hashtags | Links | Emoji |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Hook ≤ 210 chars, body ≤ 1,300 | 3 to 5, at the end | In the first comment | 0 to 2, as markers (▸ ↳) |
| X | ≤ 280 (a link counts as 23) | 0 to 2 | In the post or the last post of a thread | 0 to 1 |
| Instagram | Caption ≤ 2,200; the first line is the hook | 5 to 8, specific | "Link in bio", never in the caption | 0 to 3 |
| Reddit | Title ≤ 300; body plain and conversational | None | Plain links; say it's ours | None |

## Templates

**Signal (X):**
`<Country flag emoji> <Fact in one sentence.>` + line break + `<Why it matters, in one sentence.>` + line break + `Source: <name>` + link.
(Use flag emoji for MENA countries only.)

**Deployment of the week (LinkedIn):**
> Deployment of the week: <robot> at <site>, <city>.
>
> ▸ Who: <operator> with <maker>
> ▸ What it does: <one line>
> ▸ Since: <month year>
> ▸ Why it matters: <one line>
>
> It's one of <N> verified deployments in our database. Full record in the comments.
> #Robotics #<Country> #<Sector>

**Reddit roundup title:** `MENA robotics this week (<d Mon> to <d Mon>): <the strongest signal>, <second>, and 3 more`
